TSMC Posts Record $46.7B Q3 Revenue as AI Chip Demand Surges 50%

✍️ By Abhishek Pandey, WorldWire Desk  |  ⏱️ Published October 10, 2026 at 10:47 UTC  |  📰 Wire Attribution: Reuters
Photojournalism wire visual • Attributed to Reuters editorial archive
Executive Briefing: Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. reported record third-quarter consolidated revenue of NT$1.49 trillion ($46.71 billion), driven by insatiable global demand for artificial intelligence processors. The 50.9% year-on-year surge comfortably beat consensus estimates and the company's internal guidance ceiling. As the core fabrication partner for Nvidia, Apple, and AMD, TSMC's blowout sales demonstrate that hyperscaler hardware investment remains exceptionally resilient.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC) has smashed quarterly sales records, reporting third-quarter consolidated revenue of NT$1.494 trillion ($46.71 billion) on the back of relentless global demand for advanced artificial intelligence chips. Unaudited monthly disclosures released for September reached NT$511.86 billion ($16.03 billion), representing a 54.6% leap year-on-year and marking the strongest September turnover in the foundry's history. The quarterly haul comfortably surpassed the consensus LSEG SmartEstimate of NT$1.46 trillion and outperformed TSMC's upper guidance band of $45.8 billion, cementing its role as the dominant hardware bottleneck in the global AI boom.

The blowout performance comes ahead of TSMC's formal third-quarter earnings conference scheduled for October 15, where senior leadership is set to brief investors on gross margins, 2027 capital expenditure targets, and capacity ramps for cutting-edge 2-nanometer process technology. Holding more than 70% of the advanced global foundry market, TSMC serves as the sole contract manufacturer for flagship accelerators powering leading hyperscalers, including Nvidia's latest architectures and custom silicon from Apple and AMD. Despite a slight 0.6% sequential easing from August's record levels, the chipmaker's cumulative nine-month revenue reached NT$3.899 trillion—up 41.1% over the same period last year and tracking ahead of full-year growth projections.

TSMC's unprecedented top-line expansion highlights how deeply the global tech sector and institutional financial markets remain anchored to the semiconductor manufacturing corridor across the Taiwan Strait. As demand outstrips supply for advanced CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) packaging and next-generation lithography, industry watchers anticipate further pricing leverage for TSMC into the fourth quarter. Moving forward, global policymakers and corporate executives will closely track the foundry's multi-billion-dollar overseas manufacturing expansions in the United States, Japan, and Europe as nations scramble to insulate their AI supply chains against geopolitical vulnerabilities.
