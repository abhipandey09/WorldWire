US Imposes Sweeping Sanctions on ICC, Vowing to Dismantle Tribunal 'Brick by Brick'

✍️ By Abhishek Pandey, WorldWire Desk  |  ⏱️ Published October 10, 2026 at 10:57 UTC  |  📰 Wire Attribution: Reuters
Photojournalism wire visual • Attributed to Reuters editorial archive
Executive Briefing: The United States has designated the International Criminal Court as a sanctioned entity, escalating an unprecedented campaign to cripple the institution's global operations and financial access. The move triggers intense diplomatic friction between Washington and its European allies while delivering a profound shock to the international legal order.

WASHINGTON/THE HAGUE — In an unprecedented escalation against the international legal architecture, the United States has levied comprehensive institutional sanctions against the International Criminal Court (ICC), designating the Hague-based tribunal under Executive Order 14203. Announced by US Secretary of State Marco Rubio, the measures block all American property and interests belonging to the court and bar US individuals and companies from conducting transactions with the institution, threatening to sever its essential banking, software, and administrative lifelines. The US Treasury Department instituted a 180-day wind-down license to permit member states to negotiate terms and halt existing inquiries, warning that failure to curb the court will trigger full economic isolation.
